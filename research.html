Thesis research · 2025 – 2026

Genetic characteristics of a Vietnamese medicinal plant

My bachelor thesis studied Xa den (Ehretia asperula), a Vietnamese native medicinal plant. I used plant tissue culture, chromosome counting and karyotyping, genome size estimation with flow cytometry, and nucleic acid isolation from different tissues.

Looking ahead · Master’s study from 2027

From foundational genetics to natural product biosynthesis

My thesis began with a goal: to find the genes behind the biosynthesis of a valuable secondary metabolite in Xa den (Ehretia asperula), so that it could one day be produced at industrial scale. Because little foundational genetic information exists for this species, I shifted the thesis to its basic genetic characterization, to build that groundwork first.

That experience strengthened my interest in natural product biosynthesis, molecular genetics, bioinformatics and synthetic biology, and in applying molecular techniques to real-world crop improvement. I hope to pursue a Master’s degree in this field in 2027, with the long-term goal of continuing to a PhD.
