Thesis research · 2025 – 2026

Genetic characteristics of a Vietnamese medicinal plant

My bachelor thesis studied Xa den (Ehretia asperula), a Vietnamese native medicinal plant. I used plant tissue culture, chromosome counting and karyotyping, genome size estimation with flow cytometry, and nucleic acid isolation from different tissues.
