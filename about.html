About

I am Phi-Hung Nguyen, a recent Bachelor of Science graduate of the University of Science, Vietnam National University of Ho Chi Minh City. I study plant genes and how to apply them to improve crops and to produce valuable compounds.

My bachelor thesis characterized the genetics of Xa den (Ehretia asperula), a Vietnamese native medicinal plant, using tissue culture, chromosome counting and karyotyping, genome size estimation by flow cytometry, and nucleic acid isolation. Before that I worked as an R&D intern at a plant tissue culture laboratory. Alongside research I have taught chemistry, biology and English as a teaching assistant, tutor and volunteer.

If you work on plant genetics or biotechnology and think we could talk, send me an email.
